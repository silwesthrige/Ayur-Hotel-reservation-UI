Our Story

Three generations, one shoreline

Ayur Retreat began as a single physician's clinic in 1988. Today the same family oversees nine treatment pavilions, a resident team of Vaidyas, and a kitchen that treats food as the first medicine.

Home / About
Philosophy

Treatment, not a treatment menu

Most resorts offer Ayurveda as a spa category. We do the opposite: your stay begins with a full consultation, a pulse reading, and a programme designed for your dosha — not a price tier.

Guests rarely choose their own treatments here. Instead, our physicians prescribe a sequence, adjusted every few days as your body responds — the same approach used in our founder's original clinic in Galle.

The Property

Rooms built for a slower pace

Twenty-two suites across three low-rise wings, each facing either the treatment gardens or the shoreline — no two identical.

Wing A

Garden Suites

Wing B

Wing C
