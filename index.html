A tradition older than the hotel itself

Ayur Beach Villa was founded by a family of Ayurvedic physicians whose practice spans three generations. Every therapy on our programme traces back to classical texts — Charaka Samhita among them — adapted, not diluted, for the guest of today.

Nine treatment pavilions sit along the shoreline, each tuned to a single dosha discipline, so your programme is built around your constitution rather than a fixed spa menu.

Signature Programmes

Therapies rooted in the tridosha

Every stay opens with a consultation with a resident Vaidya, who prescribes a programme matched to your Vata, Pitta or Kapha constitution.

01 — Panchakarma

Deep Detoxification

A supervised, multi-day cleanse combining oil therapy, steam and herbal purgation to reset digestion and clear accumulated toxins.

02 — Abhyanga

Synchronised Oil Massage

Warm herbal oils applied by two therapists in mirrored rhythm, easing tension held in muscle and joint alike.

03 — Shirodhara

Continuous Oil Flow

A slow, unbroken stream of warmed oil across the forehead — the treatment most guests describe as the one that quiets the mind.

04 — Nasya

Nasal Therapy

Herbal oil administered through the nasal passage to clear the upper respiratory channels and sharpen sensory clarity.

05 — Yoga & Pranayama

Guided Practice

Sunrise sessions on the shoreline deck, sequenced to complement whatever therapy your body is moving through that week.

06 — Physician Consult

Ongoing Diagnosis

Pulse-reading consultations every few days, adjusting oils, diet and pacing as your body responds to treatment.
